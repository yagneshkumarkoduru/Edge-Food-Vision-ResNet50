# Evidence Register: Conformal Prediction Engine Validation

**Project:** Edge-Food-Vision-ResNet50
**Date:** 2026-11-14
**Scope:** V2 static split-conformal calibration, V3 online C-TTA tracker, dynamic early-exit FLOP model, PAQ compression and latency claims.

Every number below carries one tag:

- **MEASURED** - produced by executing code in this repository on synthetic numpy data and recording the output.
- **SIMULATION** - produced by a stochastic simulator whose internal parameters are assumptions, not hardware measurements.
- **MODEL** - a hardcoded literal or analytic assumption with no executed measurement behind it.

Validation harness: `src/validate_conformal_engine.py`
Artifact: `results/conformal_validation.json`
Tests: `tests/test_conformal_engine.py` (13 tests, all passing)

## 3. Measured results (this validation)

Source: `results/conformal_validation.json`, generated by `src/validate_conformal_engine.py`.

### 3.1 Static split-conformal on exchangeable (clean) data - **MEASURED**

| Quantity | Value |
|---|---|
| Target coverage (alpha = 0.05) | 95.00% |
| Measured coverage | 100.00% |
| Mean prediction-set size | 1.00 / 10 classes |
| q_hat | 0.3119 |
| Calibration / test samples | 400 / 600, seed 42 |

On clean exchangeable data the quantile level formula behaves correctly;
coverage meets and exceeds the 95% target with singleton sets.

### 3.2 Static versus C-TTA under drift (seed 42) - **MEASURED**

Stream: 1000 steps, 150-step clean calibration segment, `two_phase` drift
schedule rising to a realized maximum drift of 0.899.

| Quantity | Static | Online C-TTA (gamma = 0.01) |
|---|---|---|
| Overall coverage (post-calibration) | 28.00% | 100.00% |
| Clean-window coverage | 98.00% | 100.00% |
| Severe-window coverage (final quarter) | 2.00% | 100.00% |
| Final 200-step coverage | - | 100.00% |
| Mean prediction-set size, severe window | - | 1.29 / 10 |
| Final q_t | 0.3124 (fixed) | 0.8124 |

The static threshold loses nearly all coverage once drift sets in. The online
tracker restores coverage to 100% while keeping prediction sets small on this
stream. It overshoots the 95% target rather than settling at it.

### 3.3 Seed sweep (seeds 0-4, gamma = 0.01) - **MEASURED**

| Quantity | Mean | Std |
|---|---|---|
| Static severe-window coverage | 0.80% | 0.57% |
| C-TTA severe-window coverage | 100.00% | 0.00% |
| C-TTA overall coverage | 100.00% | 0.00% |
| C-TTA mean set size, severe window | ~1.3 | - |

The qualitative result (static collapse, C-TTA recovery) is stable across
seeds. The exact static collapse magnitude is seed-dependent and never equals
the published 40.00%.

### 3.4 Gamma sensitivity of the C-TTA tracker (seed 42) - **MEASURED**

| gamma | Overall coverage | Severe-window coverage | Final q_t | Mean set size, severe window |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.002 | 34.94% | 0.00% | 0.05 (floor) | 0.00 |
| 0.005 | 71.65% | 4.00% | 0.05 (floor) | 0.04 |
| 0.01 | 100.00% | 100.00% | 0.81 | 1.29 |
| 0.02 | 100.00% | 100.00% | 0.99 (ceiling) | 8.18 |

The tracker's realized coverage is highly sensitive to the step size. Too
small a gamma collapses q_t to its lower clip and coverage fails; too large a
gamma pins q_t to its upper clip and the prediction sets blow up to nearly
every class. The published 94.50% figure corresponds to no measured gamma on
this stream. This sensitivity is a genuine engineering finding: the C-TTA
update requires per-deployment tuning of gamma, and the "provably maintains
95% coverage" framing in the paper is an asymptotic statement, not what the
implementation realizes on a finite drifting stream.

## 4. Test suite

`tests/test_conformal_engine.py`, 13 tests, all passing
(`.venv/Scripts/python.exe -m pytest tests/ -q` -> `13 passed`).

Coverage of the suite:

- Quantile level formula (`ceil((n+1)(1-alpha))/n`) matches implementation.
- Prediction-set construction and argmax fallback (never-empty guarantee).
- Uncalibrated calibrator raises `ValueError`.
- Marginal coverage on a clean synthetic stream meets the target.
- C-TTA update direction and magnitude (cover vs miss).
- q_t clipping to [0.05, 0.99] under sustained pressure in both directions.
- Stream generator output validity (shapes, simplex rows, drift schedule).
- Static coverage collapse under drift (clean window exceeds severe window
  by more than 40 points).
- C-TTA recovery exceeds static by more than 20 points overall.
- Gamma sensitivity documented as a regression test.
- Validation artifact is written and contains the claim-audit block.

## 5. Limitations of this evidence

- All MEASURED numbers are on synthetic Dirichlet-softmax streams, not on
  Food-101 images or a trained ResNet-50. They validate the conformal and
  C-TTA math as implemented, not end-to-end model behavior.
- The drift schedule is a design choice; different schedules change the
  static collapse magnitude (the published 40.00% corresponds to some
  specific unstated schedule, not to this one).
- The early-exit FLOP figures and all PAQ size/latency figures remain
  **MODEL** literals. Validating them requires a serialized model, a
  profiler, and target hardware, none of which exist in this repository.
- The C-TTA tracker's own `evaluate_stream` method contains an internal
  stochastic simulator with hardcoded coverage probabilities; its output is
  **SIMULATION** and was not used as evidence here. The measured numbers in
  Section 3 come from driving `update_online` with realized coverage on the
  synthetic stream instead.

---

## 6. Bottom line

The conformal mathematics in the repository is implemented correctly and
behaves as theory predicts on clean data, and the qualitative paper narrative
(static calibration collapses under drift; online C-TTA recovers coverage) is
real and reproducible. None of the four headline quantitative claims
(94.50% coverage, 49.79% FLOP reduction, 87.5% size reduction, 4.71x speedup)
is a measured number; all four are hardcoded literals, and the measured C-TTA
coverage on the validated stream (100%) does not match the claimed 94.50%.
The published numbers should be re-derived from executed runs before they are
cited externally.
